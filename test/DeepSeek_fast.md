📚 DeFi Explained for Beginners
What is DeFi?
DeFi stands for Decentralized Finance - it's like traditional banking but without banks, using blockchain technology instead.

🔑 Core Concepts (Bullet Points)
✅ What Makes DeFi Different:

No intermediaries - No banks, no brokers, no middlemen
Open to everyone - Anyone with internet access can participate
Transparent - All transactions are visible on the blockchain
Permissionless - No approval needed to use services
Programmable - Smart contracts automate financial services
💰 Main DeFi Services:

Lending & Borrowing - Earn interest on deposits or borrow against collateral
Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs) - Trade tokens directly without intermediaries
Yield Farming - Earn rewards by providing liquidity to protocols
Staking - Lock tokens to support networks and earn rewards
Stablecoins - Cryptocurrencies pegged to stable assets like USD
🛡️ Key Technologies:

Smart Contracts - Self-executing code that powers DeFi apps
Liquidity Pools - User-provided funds that enable trading
Oracles - Services that bring real-world data to blockchain
Wallets - Tools to store and manage your crypto assets
🎯 Benefits for Users:

Higher yields - Often better returns than traditional savings
24/7 access - No banking hours or holidays
Global access - Available anywhere with internet
Self-custody - You control your assets, not a bank
⚠️ Important Risks:

Smart contract risk - Code bugs can lead to fund loss
Impermanent loss - Risk when providing liquidity
Market volatility - Crypto prices can swing dramatically
Regulatory uncertainty - Laws are still evolving
