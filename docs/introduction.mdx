## What is KiKo?

**KiKo** is a Labs project that deeply integrates large language models (LLMs) into the Web3 trading ecosystem.

We believe every AI model has its unique strengths. KiKo integrates mainstream models such as **DeepSeek**, **Grok** (xAI), and GPT series, orchestrating them based on their capabilities. Each model plays a specific role—whether it's information retrieval, risk analysis, or market assessment—helping traders quickly access critical information that influences market trends.

## What Problem Are We Solving?

Traditional crypto trading interfaces are often bloated and complex. You need to switch between multiple platforms, manually query data, and understand obscure on-chain metrics just to make a decision.

KiKo's answer is: **Replace interface complexity with natural language**.

Instead of learning a complex trading interface, you only need to describe your needs in everyday language. AI will orchestrate the appropriate tools to complete the entire workflow, from information gathering to on-chain execution.

## Agent Mode

KiKo supports an **Agent-driven mode**: AI does more than just answer your questions; it acts as your personal Agent to proactively execute tasks.

- Different models collaborate to automatically decompose complex instructions.
- Natural language acts as commands, with no need for manual parameter configuration.
- The Agent monitors markets, executes strategies, and sends alerts on your behalf.

This makes LLMs your true trading partners, not just a chat window.

## Core Capabilities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Information Analysis Platform">
    Real-time aggregation of multi-dimensional market signals covering all layers that influence price trends:

    - **Market Data**: Real-time tracking of price, volume, liquidity, and changes.
    - **Social Intelligence**: Discovery and keyword monitoring on Farcaster and X (Twitter).
    - **Prediction Markets**: Analysis of collective expectations and event probabilities from Polymarket.
    - **News & Insights**: Breaking news and in-depth articles in the crypto space.
    - **On-chain Data**: Tracking early buyers, monitoring "smart money," and background analysis of project teams.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trade Execution System">
    KiKo features its own execution engine, synchronizing the latest trading paths on supported chains in real-time:

    - **Smart Swap**: Initiated via natural language, featuring automatic routing optimization across multiple chains (Base, Ethereum, Solana).
    - **Copy Trading**: Automatically mirror the on-chain trade behavior of specified wallets.
    - **Risk Scan**: Automatically detect contract security before trading to identify honeypots, rugs, and other traps.
    - **Wallet Analysis**: P&L tracking, historical behavior analysis, and portfolio insights.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multi-Model Orchestration">
    KiKo isn't bound to a single model; it intelligently selects the most suitable model for the task:

    - **DeepSeek**: Deep reasoning and complex analysis scenarios.
    - **Grok** (xAI): Real-time X data, web searches, and multi-modal understanding.
    - **GPT Series**: General tasks and tool orchestration.

    The strengths of each model are fully utilized in their corresponding scenarios.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why use AI for trading?">
    Trading is essentially about information asymmetry. KiKo allows you to complete security scans, cross-chain price comparisons, social intelligence, and on-chain analysis all before making a decision—compressing what would normally take lots of time across multiple platforms into a single conversation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my private key safe?">
    KiKo uses **Privy** for key management. Your private key is controlled and managed encrypted with HSM by Privy. KiKo itself cannot access it. Every transaction requires your explicit confirmation, and KiKo will not initiate any signature without authorization.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which blockchains are supported?">
    Currently, we primarily support **Base**, **Ethereum**, and **Solana**. You can specify the chain in chat, and the system will automatically recognize and switch networks. We are continuously expanding supported chains.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
