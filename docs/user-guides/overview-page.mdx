The Overview page is KiKo's macro market data dashboard. We integrate real-time data from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com) to help you quickly grasp the overall health and capital flow of the cryptocurrency market.

---

## Core Market Metrics

At the top of the page, you can see the core macro data for the current crypto market:

- **Global Market Cap**: The current total market capitalization of all cryptocurrencies and its change over the last 24 hours.
- **24h Volume**: The total trading volume across the network in the last 24 hours, reflecting market activity.
- **Bitcoin Dominance**: Bitcoin's market cap as a percentage of the global crypto market cap. An increase usually signals capital moving to BTC for safety; a decrease often preludes "Altcoin Season."
- **Active Users**: An estimate of the number of active on-chain addresses across the network.

---

## Macro Sentiment Indicators

In addition to basic data, we provide several key market sentiment indicators:

### 1. Fear & Greed Index
Reflects the overall sentiment of market participants.
- **0-49 (Fear)**: The market is in a state of fear, potentially presenting a buying opportunity.
- **50 (Neutral)**: Market sentiment is neutral.
- **51-100 (Greed)**: The market is in a state of greed, potentially indicating a correction risk.

### 2. Altcoin Season Index
Measures whether capital is favoring Bitcoin or altcoins.
- **Bitcoin Season**: Capital is primarily focused on Bitcoin.
- **Alt Season**: Capital is overflowing into altcoins, often accompanied by broad altcoin price gains.

### 3. ETH Gas Price
Real-time display of the Ethereum mainnet base transaction fee (Gwei). Helps you judge network congestion and decide if it's a good time for mainnet interactions.
