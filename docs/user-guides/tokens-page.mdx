---

## Trending Token Ranking Algorithm

The Tokens page doesn't just rank by volume or simple price gain. It uses KiKo's proprietary **Multi-factor Trending Score algorithm**.

The algorithm synthesizes the following dimensions:
- **Short-term Momentum**: 5-minute (5m) and 1-hour (1h) price changes are heavily weighted to capture newly launched tokens.
- **Mid-term Sustainability**: 6-hour (6h) and 24-hour (24h) price changes.
- **Trading Activity**: 24-hour Volume and Transaction counts (Txns).
- **Liquidity Health**: Pool Liquidity and estimated independent traders (Makers).

> **Why do we do this?**
> Traditional 24h gain leaderboards are often dominated by older tokens that have already peaked (or are about to dump). KiKo's algorithm is extremely sensitive to 5m and 1h anomalies, allowing you to see early projects just as they start their move.

## Deep Launchpad Integration

KiKo provides dedicated display and analysis for tokens launched via "Fair Launch" platforms.

### 1. Platform Branding and Direct Links
In the token list, you'll see dedicated icons for major Launchpads (e.g., Pump.fun, Clanker, Four.meme). Click the icon to jump directly to the token's official page on that platform to verify the contract or participate in early curve trading.

### 2. Social Context
KiKo automatically fetches and displays social information provided by the Launchpad, including:
- **Creator address/label**
- **Official website**
- **X (Twitter) link**
- **Farcaster link**

### 3. Launch Multiple Estimate
To help you intuitively sense a token's early explosive power, KiKo estimates the current price relative to the initial launch price (e.g., `x15`, `x120`) based on each platform's historical initial listing price.

Click the **Star** (Favorite) icon in the top right of the details page.
