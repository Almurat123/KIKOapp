KiKo integrates multi-source data and LLM capabilities to help you quickly grasp market dynamics, wallet behavior, and project backgrounds.

---

## Wallet & Token Checks

Use research mode to inspect addresses, token safety, and execution context before trading.

**Example Commands**
- `"Check 0x... risk"`
- `"What's 0x... balance?"`
- `"Analyze wallet 0x..."`

---

## Market Data Queries

KiKo accesses real-time data from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal, allowing you to query on-chain market conditions anytime.

**Example Commands**
- `"How much is ETH now?"`
- `"Check 0x...'s liquidity and market cap"`

---

## Web Research

KiKo has web research capabilities to inspect specific projects, docs, and public webpages.

**Example Commands**
- `"What are the new features of Uniswap v4?"`
- `"Analyze https://example.xyz"` (AI summarizes webpage content automatically)
