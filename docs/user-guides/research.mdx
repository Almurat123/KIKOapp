## Topic Analysis & Social Sentiment

### X (Twitter) Topic Analysis (Grok Exclusive)

If you use the **Grok** model, you can leverage its powerful real-time X (Twitter) data access.

Grok can quickly summarize the latest conversation context about a token or event on X. This saves you significant time by telling you in plain language "why this token is pumping/dumping" or "what people are debating."

**Example Commands**
- `"What do people think about $DEGEN on X?"`
- `"Summarize today's discussion on ETH ETFs"`

### Farcaster Social Search

For other models, KiKo features a built-in Farcaster search tool. AI searches the Farcaster network for specific keywords or tokens and answers your questions based on the retrieved posts.

**Example Commands**
- `"What's the trending topic on Farcaster right now?"`
- `"Search for Farcaster posts about new tokens on Base"`

## Market Data Queries

KiKo accesses real-time data from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal, allowing you to query on-chain market conditions anytime.

**Example Commands**
- `"What tokens are trending?"`
- `"How much is ETH now?"`
- `"Top gainers on Base today?"`
- `"Check 0x...'s liquidity and market cap"`

---

## Prediction Markets (Polymarket)

Query real-time probabilities from Polymarket to understand market consensus on macroeconomics, politics, or crypto events based on real capital bets.

**Example Commands**
- `"What are the most popular predictions on Polymarket?"`
- `"What's the probability of a Fed rate cut next month?"`

---

## News & Web Research

KiKo has real-time web search capabilities to find the latest crypto news or research specific projects.

**Example Commands**
- `"What’s the big crypto news today?"`
- `"What are the new features of Uniswap v4?"`
- `"Analyze https://example.xyz"` (AI summarizes webpage content automatically)
