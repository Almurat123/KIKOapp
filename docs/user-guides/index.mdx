## Core Concepts

<Tip>
**Chat is trade**: Describe your needs in natural language; KIKO will execute trades for you.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Security first**: KIKO automatically scans token risks before trading to protect your funds.
</Info>

<Warning>
**Risk notice**: Crypto trading carries risk. Do your own research (DYOR). KIKO does not provide investment advice.
</Warning>
