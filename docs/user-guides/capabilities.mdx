KiKo is a conversational on-chain terminal. Describe your needs in natural language, and AI will invoke the appropriate tools to complete on-chain operations or information queries. Here is a comprehensive look at what we currently support.

---

## ⚡ Swap Trading

Initiate swaps using natural language without manually filling out forms. AI automatically recognizes tokens, amounts, and chains, routing them to the optimal price.

**Example Commands**
- `"Buy ETH with 100 USDC"`
- `"Sell all my PEPE"`
- `"Buy DEGEN with 50 USDC on Base, 1% slippage"`

**Supported Scope**
- Chains: Ethereum, Base, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana, and other major networks.
- DEX Aggregation Routing: Automatically find the best path via [0x Protocol](https://0x.org).
- Launchpads: [Clanker](https://clanker.world), [Zora](https://zora.co), [FourMeme](https://four.meme) (EVM); [PumpFun](https://pump.fun), [BonkFun](https://bonk.fun) (Solana).

<Tip>
  Selling an ERC-20 token for the first time requires an approval (Approve); subsequent trades for the same token do not require repeating this action.
</Tip>

See [Swap Trading](/user-guides/swap-trading) for more details.

---

## 🔁 Copy Trading

Automatically mirror the buy and sell actions of target wallets. The system listens in the background in real-time — no need for manual monitoring.

**Example Commands**
- `"Copy wallet 0x..., buy 100 USDC each time"`
- `"Follow 0x..., take profit 50%, stop loss 20%"`

**Core Parameters**: Target wallet, buy amount, TP/SL, chain, mirror sell toggle, safety filters (min MC / liquidity).

See [Copy Trading](/user-guides/copy-trading) for more details.

---

## 🌉 Cross-chain Swaps

Transfer assets across chains via the [LI.FI](https://li.fi) bridge aggregation protocol, avoiding manual interactions with bridge interfaces. AI automatically matches your wallet address on the target chain.

**Example Commands**
- `"Move my ETH from Mainnet to Solana"`
- `"Bridge 100 USDC from Base to Arbitrum"`

**Supported Assets**: Native tokens (ETH, SOL) and major stablecoins (USDC, USDT).

---

## 🛡️ Risk Scanning

Perform security scans on token contracts before trading to identify common risks.

**Example Commands**
- `"Is this token safe? 0x..."`
- `"Scan 0x... for contract risks"`

**Checks**: Honeypot, high taxes, ownership renounced status, liquidity lock status, blacklist mechanisms, etc.

| Risk Level | Score | Recommendation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Safe | 0–30 | Low risk, safe to trade |
| Medium | 30–70 | Risk exists, proceed with caution |
| High Risk | 70–100 | High risk, recommended to avoid |

You can enable "Auto-scan before trading" in settings to let the system actively block swaps with high risk.

See [Risk & Security](/user-guides/risk-and-security) for more details.

## 📊 Market Data

Query real-time on-chain market data sourced from [DexScreener](https://dexscreener.com) and [GeckoTerminal](https://geckoterminal.com).

**Queryable Content**
- Real-time price, 24h change, volume, market cap.
- Trending token rankings (by chain or network-wide).
- Historical price (e.g., `"What was the price of BTC on 2024-01-01?"`).
- Gas fees (e.g., `"What is the current ETH Gas?"`).
- Detailed token info: contract address, liquidity, holder distribution.

**Example Commands**
- `"How much is ETH now?"`
- `"Check 0x...'s liquidity and market cap"`

---

## 🔍 Wallet Analysis

Enter any wallet address, and AI will analyze its on-chain transaction behavior and profit performance.

**Analyzeable Content**
- Balance and token list.
- Historical realized PnL and win rate.
- Recent transaction records.
- "Smart Money" tracing: Find early buyers of a specific token.

**Example Commands**
- `"Analyze wallet 0x...'s PnL performance"`
- `"What's 0x...'s win rate on Base?"`
- `"Who were the earliest buyers of $MEME?"`

---

## 🌐 Research & General Knowledge

KiKo has built-in multi-source information retrieval capabilities to answer questions about the crypto space and beyond.

**Scope**
- **Web Search**: Real-time internet search for latest info, news, and project intros.
- **Deep Web Analysis**: Enter a project's official URL to automatically extract team info, Tokenomics, GitHub, and social links.
- **Comprehensive Research**: Synthesize multiple data sources to answer complex research questions.

**Example Commands**
- `"What new features does Uniswap v4 have?"`
- `"Analyze this project: https://example.xyz"` (AI will summarize webpage content)
- `"What's the big crypto news today?"`

KiKo's capabilities are constantly expanding. If you're unsure if an action is supported, just ask in natural language:

> `"Can you perform technical analysis?"`
