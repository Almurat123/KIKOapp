Welcome to KiKo! This guide covers the quick start flow that is currently implemented and directly usable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1) Sign in (Create your KiKo wallet)">
    KiKo uses **Privy** for authentication. Supported methods:

    - **Google Sign-in**
    - **Farcaster Sign-in**
    - **Passkey** (Face ID / Touch ID / Windows Hello, etc.)

    After sign-in, the system automatically creates an **Embedded Wallet** for you—no seed phrase to remember.
  </Step>

  <Step title="2) Fund your wallet (Start with Base)">
    To complete your first trade, fund your KiKo wallet (or ensure it already has funds).

    - **Base (recommended)**: Low gas, beginner-friendly
    - **Ethereum**: Gas may be higher
    - **Solana**: Any trade requires **SOL** for fees (keep at least **0.005 SOL**)

    <Tip>
      Not sure how? Just ask: "Which chains do I have balance on? Give me my deposit address."
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="3) Run a query (Verify you're ready)">
    In the chat, try:

    - "Show me my wallet balance"
    - "What's the price of ETH?"
    - "Top 5 tokens by volume on Base?"
  </Step>

  <Step title="4) Make your first small Swap (Start small)">
    Enter your trade intent directly, e.g.:

    - "Buy ETH with 20 USDC"
    - "On Base, buy DEGEN with 50 USDC"

    The system shows a trade card for you to review (price, slippage, price impact, risk notice). Depending on your settings and execution mode, you may need to confirm manually, or it may execute directly with Fast Swap Mode enabled.
  </Step>

  <Step title="5) Scan unfamiliar tokens before trading">
    Before trading, send the address:

    - "Is this token safe? 0x..."
    - "Scan this token for risks before buying: 0x..."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  KiKo does not provide investment advice. Crypto assets are volatile—only invest what you can afford to lose.
</Warning>
