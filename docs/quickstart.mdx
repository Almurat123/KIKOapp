<Steps>
  <Step title="Login and Create Wallet">
    KiKo supports multiple login methods: **Google**, **Farcaster**, **Email**, and **Twitter (X)**.

    KiKo uses [**Privy**](https://docs.privy.io) for wallet creation and key management. After logging in, a dedicated non-custodial wallet will be automatically generated for you.

    <Info>
      **Your wallet always belongs to you.** Privy provides HSM-level encryption for private keys. Even if KiKo stops providing services, your wallet remains secure on Privy's servers and **cannot be lost**. You can export your private key at any time:
      - Export from the KiKo **Settings page**.
      - Visit the [Privy Website](https://privy.io) to access and export via linked apps.

      **KiKo has no right to access your private keys or any account information.**
    </Info>

    Your account includes two wallet types:

    | Network | Description |
    |---------|-------------|
    | **EVM** | Supports Base, Ethereum, and other EVM-compatible chains. |
    | **Solana** | A separate Solana wallet address. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund Your Wallet">
    Top up your wallet to start trading. We recommend using the **Base network** for its extremely low gas fees.

    | Network | Minimum Recommended Balance |
    |---------|-----------------------------|
    | Base / Ethereum | Small amount of ETH for gas |
    | Solana | At least **0.005 SOL** for fees |

    You can fund your wallet via exchange withdrawals, cross-chain bridges, or direct purchases. If you have questions, just tell KiKo in the chat, and the AI will guide you through the process.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let AI Guide Your Setup">
    Once ready, simply talk to the AI in the chat. It will guide you through all personalized configurations, including:

    - **User Preferences**: Slippage, gas priority, default chain, etc.
    - **Copy Trading Settings**: Target addresses, amount limits, filters.
    - **Price Alerts**: Set target prices for automatic notifications.
    - **Authorize Auto-Trading**: Enable the Agent to execute trades automatically under specific conditions.
    - **Polymarket**: Enable and participate in on-chain prediction markets.
    - **Image Generation**: Create visual assets directly from chat prompts.

    > For example: Simply say _"Help me set up copy trading"_ or _"Set an alert for ETH price"_, and the AI will guide you step-by-step.
  </Step>
</Steps>
